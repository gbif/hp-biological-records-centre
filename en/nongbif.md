## Non-GBIF Data

### UK Butterfly Monitoring Scheme (UKBMS)
The United Kingdom Butterfly Monitoring Scheme (UKBMS) is one of the longest running insect monitoring schemes in the world. The scheme began in 1976 and now records data on over 3,000 sites per year; incorporating traditional butterfly transects, Wider Countryside Butterfly Survey (WCBS), and targeted surveys

The [UKBMS monitoring scheme](https://ukbms.org/) data is available as an occupancy dataset on [GBIF](https://www.gbif.org/dataset/1e266c3d-92ef-4d5a-8e4a-c04742c772c3). In addition to the occupancy data available via GBIF several research outputs that are produced annually (such as species abundance indices and abundance trends) are available from the [UKCEH Environmental Inforamtion Data Centre (EIDC)](https://catalogue.ceh.ac.uk/documents/571a676f-6c32-489b-b7ec-18dcc617a9f1). The raw count data from the monitoring scheme is available on [request from the UKBMS](https://ukbms.org/request-data).
